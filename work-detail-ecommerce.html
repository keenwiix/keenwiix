Problem

Online fashion shopping often feels cluttered and slow. Users abandon carts because of confusing checkouts, poor product pages, and slow load times.

  • Cluttered product pages with too many distractions
  • Slow load times on mobile devices
  • Confusing multi-step checkouts
  • Lack of product visualization

Research & Discovery

We analyzed competitors, ran user tests on existing fashion e-commerce sites, and interviewed shoppers about their pain points.

User Testing

25 sessions across 5 competitors

Analytics

Heatmaps and funnel analysis

Interviews

20 shoppers, aged 22-45

Mobile First

70% of traffic is mobile

Key Insights

  • Shoppers decide in < 3 seconds if a product page is trustworthy
  • Multiple product images are essential
  • One-page checkout reduces abandonment by 30%
  • Speed matters more than features

01 · Research Methods

Interviews

40 in-depth conversations with patients aged 25–70

Surveys

120 responses on digital health habits

Usability Tests

15 participants tested 3 competitor apps

Analytics

Funnel analysis of 50K+ sessions

02 · Key Insights

01

Patients want simple, clear information — not medical jargon.

02

Trust is built through transparency and calm visual design.

03

Accessibility is non-negotiable — many users are older adults.

04

Family involvement matters — accounts must support sharing.

05

Reminders should feel supportive, not pushy.

Project Overview

Keenwiix Wellness is a mobile companion for people who want to live more mindfully. Instead of pushing users with streak pressure and numbers, the app focuses on gentle daily rituals — mood check-ins, short meditations, and quiet habit-building.

Problem

Most wellness apps are built around pressure — streaks, numbers, guilt. Instead of supporting users, they add stress. People open them once, feel bad, and never come back.

  • Wellness apps create pressure, not calm
  • Too many features, too little focus
  • Visual noise undermines the purpose
  • Users feel judged when they miss a day

Research & Discovery

I analyzed popular wellness apps and read through hundreds of user reviews. The pattern was clear — people wanted calm, not competition. They wanted rituals, not achievements.

Competitive Analysis

Reviewed 8+ wellness apps

User Reviews

Key Insights

  • Users want encouragement, not pressure
  • Emotional check-ins are as valuable as physical tracking
  • Simplicity is the highest form of respect
  • Visual calm reduces anxiety about tracking

Solution

A calm companion built around four gentle practices — no gamification, no pressure, no noise.

Mood

Daily check-in with kind, unjudging feedback

Meditate

Short, contextual sessions — 3 to 30 minutes

Grow

Gentle habit-building without streak pressure

Rest

Quiet sleep insights, no obsessive metrics
